# M2a: the TV with brains (2026-10-02)

TCL 65QM6K (Mali-G52), `profile` build of b1fb33e (the shipped instinct genome, `BENCH genome resource`), logs streamed to `build/tv-m2a-bench.log` and `build/tv-m2a-starter.log`. Ant Farm stayed the screensaver throughout (`screensaver_components` and the 600000 ms timeout unchanged); the preview was stopped with `am force-stop`.

## The runs

| Run | Result | Target (spec 1, item 6) |
|---|---|---|
| colony1000, cost mode, 300 simulated seconds of warm-up, 60 measured | 34.55 ms per tick average, p50 34.33, p99 39.64, max 61.28 | average 25 ms or less, p99 50 ms or less |
| starter, simulation thread at 4x, 30 s | 80.0 ticks per second (target 80), 2.67 ms per tick, max 37.35 | holds 4x |
| colony1000 preview, 3D view, 3.5 minutes | 60 to 61 fps; frame p99 18 to 21.6 ms after the first window; 0 to 2 slow frames per 600; 19.9 to 20.0 ticks per second at 1x, 22 to 24.7 ms per tick, slowly rising as the colony spread | 58 fps or more, at most 1% of frames over 25 ms |

The first preview window (start-up) had 30 slow frames of 556; every later window had 2 or fewer. No crash or GL error.

Benchmark breakdown (ms per tick): surface ants 30.8, nest ants 1.9, publish 0.55, index 0.13, nest 0.13, fields 0.01. 14,240 SDF and 15,071 height queries per tick; the open-ground fast path served 39.8% of steps; heap 45 MB.

## Against the scripted colony

M1b-2b's scripted colony1000 ran at 12.3 ms per tick, with the fast path serving about 80% of steps. With brains the cost is about 2.8 times higher. The share of surface steps that need the full surface walk doubled, and those queries dominate the surface-ant time, not the network arithmetic. Brain-driven ants evidently spend far more time near rocks, stems and trail lines than the scripted ones did.

## Decision

The 3D view passes and 1x holds full speed, but colony1000 misses the 25 ms average (p99 passes). The owner decided on 2026-10-02 not to address it in M2a: 1,000 ants packed around one nest from the start is a stress test, while a naturally growing colony disperses, and the dispersed colony held 60 fps; performance will be revisited after launch if problems appear. Speeds above 1x for a 1,000-ant colony are not available until then (the starter holds 4x).

Likely levers, if needed later: why brain ants leave the open-ground fast path (the trail ring sense and stem and rock proximity), evaluating the network every third tick, cheaper senses, and fewer hidden units (the network grew from about 900 to 1,807 weights during M2a).
