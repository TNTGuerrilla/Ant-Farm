# M1b-2b: simulation cost on the TV (2026-10-01)

Measured on the TCL 65QM6K with the headless `BenchmarkActivity` from the non-debuggable `profile` build, started over adb (no remote, no screensaver settings changed; the app was uninstalled afterwards). Scenarios: `colony1000` (800 foragers, 200 diggers) and `starter` (60 foragers, 20 diggers), seed 1, cost mode with 300 simulated seconds of warm-up and 60 measured (1,200 ticks), and the starter through the simulation thread at 4x for 30 real seconds. Times are per tick, step plus publish.

The TV's own 10-minute idle timeout started Labyrinth at about 04:58, a few seconds after the tier 2 cost runs finished; the last run (tier 2, starter at 4x) ran behind it and still held 80 ticks per second.

## What it means

- **Most of M1b-2a's slowness was the debuggable build.** The same starter colony ran at about 13 ms per tick in the debuggable dream and 2.6 ms in the profile build, about five times faster with no code change. The baseline profile build already met the 1,000-ant target, with almost no margin (24.3 ms against 25).
- **Tier 1** (plain arrays, food indexed by chunk, a spatial index that clears only what it used, antenna trig once, unrolled field corners and a 4-block cache) cut the surface ants by 31% and the index by 72%, with identical results: 17.3 ms, about a third of the budget spare.
- **Tier 2** (the open-ground fast path) cut SDF evaluations by 71% and the surface ants by another 33%: 12.3 ms, half the budget spare. It changes results slightly, and the Gruter test needed `crowdLevel` 3 to 2; the crowded-switch check still has no spare seed.
- **Remaining cost** is now spread: surface ants about 10 ms, publish 0.7 ms (the snapshot fill and overlays), everything else under 0.5 ms. Memory stayed at 16 to 26 MB of heap.
- **Desktop for comparison** (colony1000): 2.67 ms baseline, 2.29 after tier 1, 1.03 after tier 2. The TV is 9 to 12 times slower than the desktop in the profile build, not 40.
